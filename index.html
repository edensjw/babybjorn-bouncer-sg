How this list was built

Scope and age rule

Search: “babybjorn bouncer” on carousell.sg, all 166 results, sorted recent. Kept only BabyBjörn bouncers themselves — no toy bars, fabric-only seats, bags or other brands — and dropped sold items.

Refreshed on 17 Nov 2026: every listing was re-opened to check it was still live. 4 had sold — 3 of them were on this page and have been removed; 3 new listings that appeared in the meantime were added and classified the same way.

Every listing photo was then inspected at full resolution and classified mesh vs fabric. Titles and descriptions were used only as a cross-check — plenty of genuine mesh bouncers never mention “mesh” anywhere in the listing, and a cream mesh seat can look like plain cotton until you zoom in.

Age is capped at roughly 12 months using two signals: Carousell's own “Listed … ago” line, and the date embedded in the listing photo's CDN path. Anything with photos older than ~13 months was cut, because older stock is more likely to be dusty or worn.

What the columns mean

  • Photos — date in the image URL. Often equals listing creation; can be newer if the seller re-uploaded pictures.
  • Age — “Listed 3 months ago” means posted then; “Bumped 2 days ago” means an older listing pushed back to the top, so treat its true age as unknown and rely on the photo date.
  • Fabric — “Mesh” only when the photo shows the perforated weave. Quilted, jersey and woven seats are labelled Fabric even if the seller's text says “breathable”.
  • Meet-up — the area the seller typed into the Deal method field, not a guaranteed handover spot.

Before you pay

  • BabyBjörn bouncers are newborn-safe, but check the harness, the spring/frame and that the fabric seat is intact and freshly washed.
  • Ask for the date of purchase and whether it was stored dry — Singapore humidity is hard on the mesh.
  • Meet in a public place, inspect before paying, and prefer Carousell's in-app chat so the conversation is on record.
  • Prices are asking prices as displayed; several are negotiable (“Make Offer”).
